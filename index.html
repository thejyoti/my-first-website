What is the Solar System?

The Solar System is made up of the Sun, eight planets, moons, asteroids and comets. It is located in the Milky Way galaxy. The Sun is at the centre of our Solar System and provides light and heat to the planets. Each planet has its own unique features. Some planets are rocky, while others are made mostly of gas or ice. Earth is the only planet currently known to support life. Scientists continue to explore space to understand more about our fascinating universe.
